Mental Well-Being Support

MENTAL WELL-BEING SUPPORT

Sometimes, you just need someone to listen.

Digital incidents can feel overwhelming. CybAram aims to provide respectful, non-clinical listening support and help people identify practical next steps.

Ask About Support

Please understand this service’s scope

This is non-clinical listening support. It is not psychotherapy, clinical counselling, medical treatment, diagnosis, crisis intervention or emergency care.

IS THIS RIGHT FOR YOU?

Support that fits your situation

For people seeking respectful, non-clinical listening support during stressful digital experiences.

  • You feel worried or overwhelmed after a digital incident.

  • You feel embarrassed and would value a respectful conversation.

  • You want to organize practical concerns and understand next steps.

WHAT SUPPORT MAY INCLUDE

Clear, practical support within an agreed scope

Activities and deliverables depend on availability, eligibility and the scope agreed before engagement.

01

A respectful space to describe a digital-safety concern.

02

Non-judgmental listening.

03

Help organizing immediate practical concerns.

04

General guidance on relevant digital-safety next steps.

05

Signposting to professional resources when available and appropriate.

THE PROCESS

What happens next

A straightforward process, with the purpose and boundaries agreed first.

01

Briefly describe the support you are seeking.

02

Understand service scope and availability.

03

Arrange a suitable conversation, if available.

04

Discuss practical concerns and possible next steps.

05

Receive professional-resource signposting where appropriate and available.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your comfort and safety come first

  • Share only what you feel comfortable explaining.

  • You do not need to provide unnecessary personal details.

  • Qualified professionals should provide clinical mental-health care.

  • If you are in immediate danger or crisis, contact appropriate local emergency services or qualified crisis support now.

  • Do not wait for a response from CybAram in an emergency.
