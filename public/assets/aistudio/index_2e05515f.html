CYBARAM / PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters.

This draft explains the intended handling of website enquiries. Business-specific details and processing arrangements must be reviewed and confirmed before publication.

Draft — requires review before publication

Not final legal advice or an approved business policy. Last draft update: 22 October 2026.

Information collected through forms

The contact form asks for your name, email address, enquiry category, subject, message and an optional relevant public URL. The contribution form asks for relevant skills, experience, interest and optional portfolio and availability information. A privacy acknowledgement is also requested.

Why this information is requested

Enquiry information is intended to help understand your request, determine relevant service or contribution options and communicate about it. Do not provide passwords, OTPs, PINs, banking credentials, full payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

How enquiries are handled

Access should be limited to people who need the information to review and respond to an enquiry. Actual internal access, handling procedures and responsible business contact must be confirmed.

Hosting and form-processing providers

This website is being built in Framer. The form delivery destination and any additional processing providers have not been configured or confirmed. Their roles and applicable privacy notices must be reviewed before forms are enabled.

Data retention and deletion

No retention period has been confirmed. CybAram must establish the actual retention, deletion and enquiry-management policy before collecting personal information through these forms.

Privacy questions and requests

The official privacy contact has not been supplied. A confirmed method for privacy enquiries and applicable access or deletion requests must be added before launch.

Security practices and limitations

Collect only necessary information, use appropriate access controls and agree suitable secure channels for any sensitive incident evidence. No transmission method is guaranteed to be risk-free; do not send credentials through this website.

Cookies and analytics

Actual analytics, cookies and third-party services must be reviewed and accurately disclosed before publication. This draft does not assert that a particular tracking or consent configuration is in use.
