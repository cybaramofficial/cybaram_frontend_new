DIGITAL EXPOSURE ASSESSMENT

Understand what information about you may be exposed online.

A practical assessment can help you understand publicly accessible information that may create privacy or digital-safety risks.

Ask About an Assessment

IS THIS RIGHT FOR YOU?

Support that fits your situation

For people who want to understand information about them that may be publicly exposed online and learn practical ways to improve privacy.

  • You want to understand your public digital footprint.

  • You are reviewing personal privacy or contact visibility.

  • You want practical recommendations, not unauthorized account access.

WHAT SUPPORT MAY INCLUDE

A practical look at your public digital footprint

A digital exposure assessment reviews relevant information accessible through lawful public sources. It does not access private accounts or restricted information.

01

Publicly accessible profile information.

02

Public search results associated with supplied identifiers.

03

Publicly visible contact details and personal-information references.

04

Public information relevant to privacy or impersonation risks.

05

Practical opportunities to reduce unnecessary exposure.

THE PROCESS

What happens next

A straightforward process, with the purpose and boundaries agreed first.

01

Initial enquiry and eligibility check.

02

Agree the purpose, scope and lawful sources.

03

Review relevant public-source information.

04

Provide a summary of relevant findings, where agreed.

05

Discuss practical privacy recommendations.

GOOD TO KNOW

Potential deliverables

  • Summary of relevant findings, if included.

  • Explanation of potential exposure.

  • Practical privacy recommendations.

  • Suggested platform reporting or removal routes.

  • Clear scope and research limitations.
