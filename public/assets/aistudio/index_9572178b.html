THE PROCESS

What happens next

A straightforward process, with the purpose and boundaries agreed first.

01

Share the concern through an official contact method.

02

Explain the situation using only necessary information.

03

Understand the relevant support options.

04

Review practical next steps and reporting resources.

GOOD TO KNOW

What to prepare

  • A brief description of what happened.

  • Relevant screenshots or messages, where safe to share.

  • Dates and approximate times.

  • Transaction references only when necessary, through an appropriate secure method.

  • Steps you have already taken. Never share passwords, OTPs, PINs or remote-access credentials.
