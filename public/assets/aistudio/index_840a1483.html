Service scope and eligibility

CybAram describes practical digital-safety guidance, public-source assessment, eligible business research, impersonation support and non-clinical listening support. Availability, eligibility, scope and deliverables must be agreed for the relevant service.

Your responsibilities

Provide accurate information about your request and authority. Share only information needed for the agreed purpose. Do not submit passwords, OTPs, PINs, banking credentials, full card details or unnecessary sensitive data.

Lawful and acceptable use

Do not request unauthorized access, security-control bypasses, credential theft, doxxing, harassment, unlawful surveillance, stalking or private relationship investigations. Business OSINT requires a legitimate, eligible organizational purpose.

Limitations of guidance

Financial recovery, investigation outcomes, discovery of every exposed item, profile removal and future safety cannot be guaranteed. The website is not a substitute for qualified legal, financial, clinical or emergency advice.

Third-party decisions and sources

Platforms, banks, reporting authorities and other third parties control their own processes, information and decisions. Public-source information may be incomplete, outdated or inaccurate and requires contextual review.

Enquiries, fees and engagement

No official prices are confirmed. Enquiries do not automatically create an engagement. Scope, fees, billing basis, delivery arrangements and any applicable cancellation or refund terms must be confirmed before work begins.

Non-clinical support

Mental Well-Being Support is not therapy, clinical counselling, diagnosis, medical treatment, crisis intervention or emergency care. Seek appropriately qualified professionals for clinical needs.
