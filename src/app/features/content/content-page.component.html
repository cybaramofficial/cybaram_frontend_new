Our Story

From Real Cases
to a Real Mission

We have seen how online scams, fraud, data misuse and impersonation attacks can affect real people — students, working professionals, small business owners, and even families. Beyond the financial loss, these incidents often leave people confused, stressed and without guidance.

These experiences inspired us to start CybAram — a space where awareness, practical support and human understanding come together to help people stay safer in the digital world.

Our Mission

Empower People.
Build a Safer Digital Society.

To create a safer digital environment by spreading awareness, providing practical support and enabling people to make informed decisions online.
